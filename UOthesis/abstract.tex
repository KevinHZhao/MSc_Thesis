The goal of this thesis is to illustrate how complex a simple
{\bfseries discrete dynamical system} of the form
\[
x_{i+1} = f(x_i)  \quad,
\]
where $f:\RR\rightarrow \RR$ is a continuous function, can be.  The
complexity is even greater if $f:\RR^k \rightarrow \RR^k$ with $k>1$.
Discrete dynamical systems are parts of many numerical algorithms.
For instance, the Newton method is a discrete dynamical system, some methods
to solve ordinary differential equations and partial differential
equations are using discrete dynamical systems, etc.  It is
therefore important to understand the behaviour of discrete
dynamical systems. %\href{http://aix1.uottawa.ca/~bdionne}{TEST}
