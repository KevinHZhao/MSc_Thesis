\nonumchapter{Bad Listed Chapter}

\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} (x,y) = x^2 y \ .
\end{equation}

\begin{defn}
  A definition\index{No Number!Definition}
\end{defn}

The numbering refers to the previous chapter and section.    That is
certainly not what we should have.

\clearpage

\setcounter{chapter}{0}
\stepcounter{chapter}   % To ensure that the section and subsection
                        % counters be reset to 0.
\renewcommand{\thechapter}{\Alph{chapter}}

\nonumchapter{Acceptable Listed Chapter}

\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} (x,y) = x^2 y \ .
\end{equation}

\begin{defn}
  A definition\index{No Number!Definition}
\end{defn}

The following code was used just before the command \verb=\nonumchapter=.

\begin{verbatim}
\setcounter{chapter}{0}
\stepcounter{chapter}   % To ensure that the section and subsection
                        % counters be reset to 0.
\renewcommand{\thechapter}{\Alph{chapter}}
\end{verbatim}

If you have numbered chapters that follow this one, you have to
manually reset the counter and the definition of the chapter number as
we did above.

To be honest, there is no reason to include numbered equations,
definitions, ... in a chapter without a number.
